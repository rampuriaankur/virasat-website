About Us

Preventive care for precious collections

We strongly believe that all kinds of preventive measures are the best and practical approach for the long-term preservation of our precious collection. We provide high-quality customised archival products to archives, museums, libraries, universities, corporate archives, cultural institutions, and private collectors across India.

Our Vision

To contribute towards safeguarding our documentary and cultural heritage by becoming a trusted partner in the preservation and long-term care of valuable collections.

Our Mission

To provide thoughtfully designed, customised and practical archival storage and preservation solutions that help institutions and individuals protect, preserve and manage their valuable collections in accordance with internationally recognised archival preservation principles and practices.

Our Objectives

  • Safeguard documents, records, photographs, artefacts and other significant collections against deterioration, damage and loss.
  • Provide customised, archival-quality storage materials and preservation solutions.
  • Promote good archival preservation practices through professional guidance and consultation.
  • Support the systematic organisation, documentation and accessibility of collections.
  • Make sustainable preservation solutions accessible to institutions, organisations and individual collectors.

Projects & Professional Experience

Experience in collection care

Virasat Solutions brings experience gained through a range of archival preservation and collection-care assignments undertaken in various capacities, including collaborations and engagements through established organisations and service providers.

The experience spans projects associated with private-sector organisations, government institutions, archives, auction houses and individual collectors, covering diverse requirements relating to the care, preservation, storage and management of valuable collections.
